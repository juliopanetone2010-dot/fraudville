Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Who we are

Fraudville is a YouTube channel (@FraudvilleTV). "Fraudville Uploader" is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to YouTube.

What the Fraudville Uploader accesses

The tool uses the YouTube Data API, with the channel owner's permission, to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the Fraudville channel and to read that channel's basic information. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's Google or YouTube account.

What we collect and store

We do not collect, sell or share personal data from viewers or anyone else. The only credential stored is the channel owner's own authorization token, kept locally on the owner's computer. You can revoke this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Fraudville Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Viewers of our videos

When you watch Fraudville on YouTube, YouTube and Google handle your data under their own policies. We only see the aggregated, anonymous statistics YouTube provides to every channel.
